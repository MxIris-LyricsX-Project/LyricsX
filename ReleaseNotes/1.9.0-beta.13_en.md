# What's New

## Fixes

- The app and its widget crashed at launch on macOS 26.3 and earlier, caused by a mistake in Apple's SDK that labels a logging API added in macOS 26.4 as available since macOS 11.
- Clicking the elapsed or remaining time under the lyrics panel's progress bar no longer skips to the previous or next song.

## Upgrading

- If the previous beta does not open on your Mac, download this version from the release page and replace the app by hand, because the broken build cannot update itself.
