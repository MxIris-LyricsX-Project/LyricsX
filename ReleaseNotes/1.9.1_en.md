# What's New

## Fixes

- Fixed a line of desktop lyrics sometimes staying blank, most often in Japanese songs with a translation.
- Automatic furigana now puts each reading over its own kanji instead of across the whole word.
- Automatic romaji in desktop lyrics no longer shifts and now fills in with the highlight.
- Widgets no longer grey out the cover or hide the small widget's title while another app is in front.
- Stepping the lyrics offset now lands on whole 100 ms, so an offset that came with the lyrics can return to 0.
- The menu bar icon no longer takes more room than the icons next to it.
