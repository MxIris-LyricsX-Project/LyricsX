# What's New

## New

- **The Apple Music-style lyrics panel has been rebuilt against Music 26** — the backdrop is now built from the cover art itself, line changes cascade one row after another, and type size steps at the same widths Music switches at.
- **Sung words rise syllable by syllable and stay risen**, instead of every word swelling and dropping back. Applies to Apple Music lyrics and to the inline-timed results from Kugou, QQ Music and some NetEase sources.
- **The panel hides its title bar in full screen**, so there is no black strip across the top any more.
- **A sharper cover in the panel.** A higher-resolution copy of the same cover is looked up in the background, and only used once it is confirmed to be the same picture.
- **The lyrics search window shows the score it sorts by**, with the cover-similarity bonus spelled out separately.
- **Apple Music lyrics keep their per-word timing**, which the parser used to discard.

## Fixes

- A lyric long enough to wrap showed its second line above its first.
- Saturated cover art could wash the panel's background bright red.
- Word-timed lyrics lost their bounce on line changes.
- The karaoke sweep drifted out of alignment with the text during partial repaints.
- The panel no longer drags the whole system's frame rate down, or sits at 12% CPU with nothing playing.
- Playback control no longer starts a new process for every play, pause or state check.
