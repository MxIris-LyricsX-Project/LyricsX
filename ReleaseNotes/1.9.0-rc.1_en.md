# What's New

## New

- New Lyrics translation in Preferences → Lab fills in missing translations, using System Translation on macOS 26 or an OpenAI-compatible service.
- A Display language setting chooses which translation the desktop lyrics, the Apple Music-style lyrics window and the widget show.
- Lines in the Apple Music-style lyrics window respond to hovering and clicking as they do in Music.

## Improvements

- Unplayed lines and translations in the Apple Music-style lyrics window now look as they do in Music.
- The Apple Music-style lyrics window no longer keeps the Mac busy while paused or idle, and its background holds still while paused.
- Widgets follow the app's settings, including Prefer bilingual lyrics, instead of having options of their own.
- The large and extra-large widgets show as many whole lyric lines as fit.
- The widget's playback buttons no longer open the app.
- Widget lyrics change closer to when each line is sung.

## Fixes

- Lines without word timing now light up all at once in the Apple Music-style lyrics window instead of word by word.
- Fixed album artwork spilling out of the extra-large widget.
- Wrong Lyrics no longer deletes lyrics files you keep beside your music.
- Fixed Search Lyrics applying a different result than the one selected.
- Lyrics chosen in Search Lyrics or with Next Lyrics Candidate are no longer saved to a song that started playing in the meantime.
- Fixed rejected lyrics coming back, and lyrics in Music being rewritten, after a search was interrupted.
- Fixed a crash in the lyrics window when its font is no longer installed.
