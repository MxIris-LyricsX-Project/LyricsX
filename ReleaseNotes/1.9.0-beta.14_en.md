# What's New

## Fixes

- On macOS 26 and later, the menu bar lyrics no longer keep the CPU busy while the line sits still, and use less of it while scrolling.
- Songs playing in Spotify now show their cover in the lyrics panel.
- With Spotify, the lyrics panel no longer shows a nonsense remaining time or a progress bar stuck at the start, and lyrics search no longer misjudges the song's length.

## Acknowledgements

Thanks to @SmallSquare for (#198), which tracked down the menu bar's CPU cost and was the basis for its fix.
