# What's New

## New

- Turn on **Hide menu bar lyrics when paused** in **Preferences → Lab** to hide the menu bar lyrics while music is paused.

## Improvements

- The highlight in the Apple Music-style lyrics window now follows each sung syllable, as in Music.
- The large widget now shows the song's cover.
- **Disable lyrics when: Music is not playing** no longer hides the menu bar lyrics.

## Fixes

- The **Lyrics Offset** row in the menu bar menu lines up with the other items again on macOS 27.
