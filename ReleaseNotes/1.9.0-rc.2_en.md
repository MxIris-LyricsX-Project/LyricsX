# What's New

## Upgrading

- If you added desktop widgets in an earlier beta, remove them and add them again so they all update on time.

## Improvements

- The progress bar in the Apple Music-style lyrics window now looks and works like Music's, growing while you hold it.

## Fixes

- Fixed desktop widgets going black or staying on the previous version after an update until you logged out.
- The widget no longer shows the last song as playing after you quit the app.
- **Wrong Lyrics** and **Disable Lyrics for Entire Album** no longer erase lyrics you added in Music yourself.
- Fixed lyrics reappearing after **Disable Lyrics for Entire Album**, or after **Wrong Lyrics** while **Next Lyrics Candidate** was searching.
- The Apple Music-style lyrics window no longer shows another song's cover after you skip songs quickly.
- The menu bar no longer shows the previous song's last line until the next song's lyrics begin.
- The menu bar lyrics now also hide while paused when **Disable lyrics when: Music is not playing** is on.
- Fixed a crash when dragging text from another app onto the list in **Preferences → Source**.
