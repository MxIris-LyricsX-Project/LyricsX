# What's New

## New

- Menu bar lyrics can show karaoke progress in a colour of your choice: Preferences → Lab (macOS 26 or later).
- The lyrics offset can be set for each player and output device: Preferences → General → Per Player & Output….
- The status menu's Local Lyrics submenu switches between the track's embedded lyrics and lyrics files.
- Single lyrics sources can be switched off in Preferences → Source.
- Preferences → Lab shows whether Apple Music accepts your User Token.

## Improvements

- The higher-resolution cover in the Apple Music-style window now also works for NetEase Cloud Music tracks.
- A widget on the desktop no longer keeps hundreds of megabytes of memory busy.

## Fixes

- Fixed no lyrics being found for the first song after launch when an Apple Music token is set.
- Lyrics now follow when you seek while playback is paused.
- The karaoke highlight in desktop lyrics no longer shrinks or flickers when you pause and resume.
- Dragging desktop lyrics is no longer interrupted by a line change, and works across displays.
- Japanese words in lines that mix English and Japanese now get furigana and romaji.
- Automatic furigana is back for words such as ヶ月, 関ヶ原 and 〆切.
